# Technical specification: Rapid chargers for the municipal vehicle fleet
Contracting authority: Municipality of Groenvelde, Fleet Management Department

1 General
1.1 The municipality operates around 210 vehicles, including refuse collection trucks, vans and passenger cars.
1.2 By 2030, at least 80 % of the light fleet is to be electric under the municipal climate plan.
1.3 The chargers will be installed at the central works yard, which is connected to the grid at 10 kV.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 Each charging station shall have two charge points.
2.2 A charging power of 240 kW per charge point is required.
2.3 The chargers must support output voltages from 200 V to 1000 V.
2.4 Maximum current shall be at least 400 A.
2.5 The enclosure shall be rated IP55.
2.6 The operating temperature range shall be at least -25 °C to +45 °C.
2.7 The chargers must communicate with the municipal back office via OCPP 2.1.
2.8 ISO 15118-2 shall be supported for Plug & Charge.
2.9 Card payment at the charger is desirable for occasional use by visitors.
2.10 The efficiency should be at least 95 %.
2.11 An availability of 97 % per year shall be guaranteed.
2.12 The supplier's service technicians shall respond on site within 24 hours.
2.13 Cable length should be at least 6 m.

3 Standards
3.1 The chargers shall comply with IEC 61851-1.
3.2 Conformity with IEC 61851-23 is mandatory.
3.3 Impact resistance should be at least IK10 according to IEC 62262.

4 Contract terms
4.1 Delivery shall take place within 16 weeks of the order.
4.2 The warranty period shall be 24 months.
4.3 Penalties for late delivery are capped at 5 % of the contract value.
4.4 Invoices will be paid within 60 days.
4.5 Liability shall be limited to the contract value.
4.6 Documentation must be provided in English.
4.7 Spare parts should be available for at least 8 years after commissioning.

5 Submission
5.1 Tenders must be submitted through the municipality's e-procurement platform by 17 February 2027, 14:00.
5.2 Bidders must demonstrate at least three comparable fleet charging projects.
5.3 Proof of professional liability insurance shall be enclosed.
