# Technical specification: DC charging infrastructure for an electric van depot
Contracting authority: Rijnmond Stadslogistiek B.V.

1 General
1.1 Rijnmond Stadslogistiek operates a fleet of 140 delivery vans from its depot in the Waalhaven area, of which 60 are already battery-electric.
1.2 The depot has a grid connection of 1.5 MVA, of which roughly 400 kVA is currently used for buildings and AC chargers.
1.3 Vehicles usually return between 18:00 and 21:00 and leave again at around 05:30.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 Each charger shall provide two charge points.
2.2 The charging power per charge point shall be at least 150 kW.
2.3 The output voltage range must cover 150 V to 920 V.
2.4 The maximum output current per charge point should be not less than 250 A.
2.5 Peak efficiency must be at least 96 %.
2.6 The chargers must have an ingress protection rating of at least IP55.
2.7 Chargers should operate between -20 °C and +40 °C.
2.8 Communication with the fleet management system shall use OCPP 1.6J.
2.9 Support for Plug & Charge according to ISO 15118-20 is required.
2.10 Charging cables should be 7.5 m long so that they reach vans parked nose-in.
2.11 Charger availability should be at least 97 %.
2.12 In the event of a fault, a service technician must be on site within 4 hours.
2.13 Ad hoc card payment is not needed at the depot, as the site is closed to the public.

3 Standards
3.1 The chargers shall be certified to IEC 61851-23.
3.2 Connectors must comply with IEC 62196-3.
3.3 Emission limits according to EN 61000-6-4 should be met.

4 Contract terms
4.1 Delivery and commissioning shall be completed within 14 weeks of the purchase order.
4.2 A warranty of 36 months is required.
4.3 Late delivery penalties are 1 % of the order value per week, capped at 5 %.
4.4 Payment term: 45 days after invoice date.
4.5 Liability is limited to EUR 1 million per event.
4.6 Training of two days for depot staff should be included.
4.7 Documentation shall be provided in English.

5 Submission
5.1 Offers shall be sent by e-mail to the procurement team no later than 31 January 2027.
5.2 Bidders must provide references from at least two fleet depot installations.
5.3 Offers shall remain valid for 90 days.
