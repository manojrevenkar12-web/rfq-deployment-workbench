# Technical specification: Ultra-fast charging hub at a motorway service area
Contracting authority: Hanseatic Highway Services GmbH

1 General
1.1 The service area is located on a motorway section with an average daily traffic of about 64,000 vehicles.
1.2 The hub will comprise eight charging stations under a canopy carrying a photovoltaic system of 250 kWp.
1.3 The site is connected to the medium-voltage grid; the transformer station is provided by the contracting authority.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 Each charging station shall provide two charge points.
2.2 The charging power per charge point shall be at least 480 kW in order to serve the next generation of 800 V vehicles.
2.3 The output voltage shall range from 150 V to 1000 V.
2.4 The maximum output current shall be at least 600 A.
2.5 Charging stations must be rated at least IP54.
2.6 Operation should be possible between -30 °C and +50 °C.
2.7 The stations must comply with German calibration law (Eichrecht).
2.8 Ad hoc payment by debit and credit card shall be possible at every station.
2.9 The stations shall support OCPP 2.0.1.
2.10 Plug & Charge according to ISO 15118-2 should be supported.
2.11 Availability of each charge point shall be at least 98 %.
2.12 Efficiency should be at least 96 %.
2.13 The service response time should not exceed 6 hours.

3 Standards
3.1 The stations shall be certified to IEC 61851-23.
3.2 Conformity with PTB-A 50.7 is required.
3.3 Electromagnetic immunity should comply with EN 61000-6-2.

4 Contract terms
4.1 All stations shall be delivered within six months of contract award.
4.2 A warranty of 60 months is required.
4.3 Delay penalties shall not exceed 10 % of the contract value.
4.4 The supplier's liability is capped at twice the contract value.
4.5 Payment shall be made within 30 days.
4.6 Documentation shall be provided in German.

5 Submission
5.1 Tenders shall be submitted by 1 March 2027 via the procurement platform.
5.2 Bidders shall provide references for at least three charging hubs with a total power of more than 2 MW each.
5.3 Tenders must remain valid for 90 days.
