# Technical specification: Procurement of a tamping machine for plain line and switches
Contracting authority: Nordic Track Maintenance Consortium AB

1 General
1.1 The consortium maintains approximately 2,300 km of track in central and northern Sweden on behalf of three regional infrastructure owners.
1.2 During winter, temperatures in the operating area regularly fall below -30 °C for several weeks.
1.3 The new machine will replace two units built in 1998 and 2001, which together have logged more than 110,000 engine hours.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 The machine shall be built for standard gauge track (1,435 mm).
2.2 The maximum permitted axle load is 22.5 tonnes.
2.3 The minimum self-propelled travel speed is 100 km/h.
2.4 Installed engine power shall be not less than 600 kW.
2.5 Engines shall meet emission stage V.
2.6 The machine must be fully operational in ambient temperatures from -40 °C to +30 °C.
2.7 The machine shall achieve a technical availability of at least 98 % during the winter season.
2.8 Fire protection should meet EN 45545-2 hazard level HL1.
2.9 Cab noise should be kept below 78 dB(A).
2.10 The machine should be able to work on gradients of up to 30 per mille.

3 Standards
3.1 The machine shall comply with EN 14033-1 and be authorised accordingly.
3.2 On-board electronic equipment shall conform to EN 50155.
3.3 Welding should be performed in accordance with EN 15085-2.

4 Contract terms
4.1 Delivery is required within 12 months of the contract award.
4.2 The supplier shall provide a full warranty of 60 months.
4.3 Liquidated damages for delay of 0.5 % of the contract value per week shall apply without an upper limit.
4.4 Payment will be made within 30 days of invoice.
4.5 Training of six days for operators and maintenance staff shall be included.
4.6 Spare parts must remain available for at least 15 years.
4.7 Documentation should be supplied in English.

5 Submission
5.1 Tenders shall be submitted electronically by 26 February 2027.
5.2 Bidders must submit a list of at least three reference machines in operation in climates with winter temperatures below -25 °C.
5.3 Tenders shall remain binding for 90 days.
