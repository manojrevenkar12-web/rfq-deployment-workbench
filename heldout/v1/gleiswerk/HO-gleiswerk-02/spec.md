# Technical specification: Supply of a rail grinding train
Contracting authority: Havnebane Infrastruktur A/S

1 General
1.1 Havnebane Infrastruktur A/S maintains approximately 640 km of standard-gauge track serving three seaports and their hinterland.
1.2 Rail grinding has so far been carried out by external contractors on roughly 180 km of track per year.
1.3 This procurement covers one self-propelled rail grinding train including training, documentation and an initial spare parts package.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 The grinding train shall be designed for a track gauge of 1435 mm.
2.2 The axle load must not exceed 22.5 t at any point of the train.
2.3 A minimum travel speed of 100 km/h in self-propelled transfer mode is required.
2.4 The total installed engine power shall be at least 700 kW.
2.5 The diesel engines should meet EU emission stage IIIB as a minimum.
2.6 The train will be stabled outdoors at coastal depots. It must be fully operational at ambient temperatures between -30 °C and +35 °C.
2.7 Fire protection must meet hazard level HL2 according to EN 45545-2.
2.8 The noise level in the driver's cab should not exceed 80 dB(A).
2.9 The contracting authority requires a technical availability of at least 97 % over the warranty period.
2.10 It is desirable that the train can operate on gradients of up to 35 ‰.

3 Standards
3.1 The vehicle shall be approved in accordance with EN 14033-1.
3.2 Wheel profiles should comply with EN 13715.
3.3 The supplier shall demonstrate RAMS management in accordance with EN 50126-1.
3.4 Our existing track recording car was built to EN 14033-3 and is not part of this procurement.

4 Contract terms
4.1 Delivery shall take place no later than 48 weeks after contract award.
4.2 The warranty period is 36 months from acceptance.
4.3 Liquidated damages for late delivery amount to 0.5 % of the contract price per week, capped at 10 % of the contract price.
4.4 The supplier's total liability is limited to twice the contract value.
4.5 Invoices will be paid within 60 days of receipt.
4.6 Documentation should preferably be supplied in English.
4.7 Tenders must remain valid for 120 days after the submission deadline.

5 Submission
5.1 Tenders must be submitted electronically via the procurement portal by 15 February 2027, 12:00 CET.
5.2 Bidders shall provide at least two references for rail grinding machines delivered within the last eight years.
5.3 Proof of product liability insurance with a minimum cover of EUR 10 million must be enclosed with the tender.
