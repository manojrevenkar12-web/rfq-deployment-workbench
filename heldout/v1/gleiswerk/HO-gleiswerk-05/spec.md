# Technical specification: Road-rail excavator for track maintenance
Contracting authority: Western Seaboard Rail Infrastructure DAC

1 General
1.1 Western Seaboard Rail Infrastructure DAC is responsible for about 520 km of mostly single-track railway along the west coast.
1.2 The current fleet comprises four road-rail machines, the oldest of which entered service in 2006.
1.3 Most maintenance work is carried out during night-time possessions of six to eight hours.

2 Technical requirements
2.1 The rail running gear must be suitable for the Irish track gauge of 1600 mm.
2.2 The maximum permissible axle load on rail is 18 t.
2.3 The machine shall travel on rail at a speed of at least 25 km/h.
2.4 Engine power should be no less than 120 kW.
2.5 The engine must comply with EU Stage V.
2.6 Machines operate in a mild but very wet maritime climate. The machine shall be operable at ambient temperatures from -15 °C to +35 °C.
2.7 Gradients of up to 20 ‰ must be negotiated with a fully loaded bucket.
2.8 Noise at the operator's position shall not exceed 78 dB(A).
2.9 A technical availability of 95 % is desirable.

3 Standards
3.1 The machine must be certified to EN 15746-1.
3.2 Compliance with EN 15746-2 is mandatory.
3.3 Welding of the rail gear frame should be carried out to EN 15085-2.

4 Contract terms
4.1 The machine shall be delivered within 9 months of contract award.
4.2 A warranty of 24 months is required.
4.3 Delay damages are payable at 1 % of the contract price per week of delay up to a maximum of 5 %.
4.4 Payment terms are 30 days from the date of a valid invoice.
4.5 Operator training of four days should be included.
4.6 All manuals shall be provided in English.

5 Tender submission
5.1 Tenders shall be submitted via the electronic tender portal no later than 12:00 on 4 March 2027.
5.2 Tenderers must provide evidence of public liability insurance of not less than EUR 6.5 million.
5.3 At least two references for road-rail machines supplied to European infrastructure managers shall be included.
