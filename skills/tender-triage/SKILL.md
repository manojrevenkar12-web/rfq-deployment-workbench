# Tender triage

## Steps

1. `tender_summary(customer, tender_id)`: the bid recommendation with reasons, knockouts, conditions,
   red flags with their source sentences, cited norms with certification status, and the variant.
2. Lead with the decision and why, in one sentence. Then the knockouts (if any), then each red flag with
   the buyer's sentence quoted and its location in the document.
3. Clarification questions go in one letter, so the buyer answers once: every requirement to clarify,
   every norm the company profile does not know.
4. Anything the agents marked "other" is outside the vocabulary: a person reads it, and if it recurs the
   kind goes to the product-feedback report.

The agents read; a person decides. Say which answers a sales engineer still has to confirm.
